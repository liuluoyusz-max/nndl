Fixed external style pool for AdaIN augmentation.

Source dataset used by the AdaIN paper: WikiArt paintings, about 80,000 images,
distributed for this line of work through the Kaggle Painter by Numbers competition.
Content images for this project stay the PACS Photo training split, not MS COCO.

This folder keeps 10 style images from the official PyTorch AdaIN demo set:
https://github.com/naoto0804/pytorch-AdaIN/tree/master/input/style

Excluded on purpose:
- sketch.png, because it is close to the PACS Sketch test domain
- cropped duplicates of mondrian.jpg and woman_in_peasant_dress.jpg
- every image under data/images, including PACS Art, Cartoon, and Sketch

Pairing uses seed 42. Each Photo training image receives one style.
